Questions

Before you ask

How fast can a mechanic reach me?+

Every listing shows a live ETA. Mobile mechanics in city areas typically arrive in 20–45 minutes; garages show the ETA if they offer pickup or roadside visits. Pick the closest one and watch the status move in My Bookings.

How does pricing work — will I be overcharged?+

No. The service charge for your problem is fixed and shown before you book. If a part is needed, the mechanic quotes the market price and fits it only after your approval. You pay the mechanic directly, after the job — MechNow never holds your money.

Do you support electric bikes and EVs?+

Yes. Use the “EV specialist” filter to see mechanics trained on electric two-wheelers and EVs — controller, battery, BMS and charging faults included. High-voltage work is only assigned to EV-trained partners.

How do I pay?+

Pay the mechanic after the job is done — UPI, cash or card, whatever they accept. There is no advance payment and no booking fee for customers in this demo.

I'm a mechanic. How do I join, and what does it cost?+

Fill the “Join as a mechanic” form above — your listing goes live instantly with a New Partner badge. Joining is free; MechNow takes a 10% commission only on completed jobs (illustrative in this demo). ID verification completes your Verified badge.
