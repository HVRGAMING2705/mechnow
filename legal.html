Privacy Policy

MechNow ("MechNow", "we", "us") is a roadside-mechanic marketplace that connects vehicle owners with independent local mechanics. This policy explains what information the MechNow web app handles and how.

Information stored on your device

The current MechNow demo runs entirely in your browser. Your bookings, mechanic listings you create, problem and location selections, and your theme preference are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not transmitted to, or stored on, any MechNow server, and there are no user accounts, analytics or advertising trackers in this demo.

Information you provide

When you book a mechanic you may optionally enter your name, phone number and a note. In the full product, these details are shared only with the mechanic you booked, so they can reach and help you. In this demo they never leave your device.

Location

MechNow does not collect GPS location in this demo. You choose your city and area manually from lists. If a future version offers device location, it will ask for your permission first and use it only to find mechanics near you.

Cookies

This demo sets no cookies. Local storage is used solely for the app functions described above, and you can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. In the full product, booking details are shared only with your chosen mechanic, and information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the safety of users and mechanics.

Children

MechNow is intended for vehicle owners and mechanics aged 18 and above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted on this page with a new "last updated" date. For privacy questions, contact the MechNow operator via the MechNow website.

The service

MechNow is a marketplace and booking tool. We connect vehicle owners with independent mechanics and garages ("Mechanics"). MechNow does not itself repair vehicles, and Mechanics are independent businesses, not employees or agents of MechNow.

Bookings, pricing and payment

  • Service charges shown in the app are fixed prices for the selected job, set by the Mechanic or the platform's standard table. Parts, if required, are extra at market rate and are fitted only after your approval.
  • Customers pay the Mechanic directly after the job is completed. MechNow charges Mechanics a platform commission on completed jobs (10% in this demo, illustrative).
  • Estimated times of arrival (ETAs) and distances are estimates, not guarantees.
  • You may cancel a booking before work begins. Repeated no-shows may lead to restricted access.

Mechanic obligations

Mechanics listing on MechNow must provide accurate information, complete identity verification before receiving a Verified badge, honour the prices they quote in the app, and perform work with reasonable skill and care. MechNow may delist Mechanics who breach these Terms or whose ratings fall below platform standards.
